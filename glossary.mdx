# Glossary

Terms specific to Tumbuh are defined here. For canonical industry terms — ERC-4626, ERC-7201, UUPS — this page links to the authoritative source rather than restating the definition.

Entries are alphabetical.

### `ADMIN_MANAGER`

A role on the [TumbuhRouter](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router) that controls the swap-percentage parameter and the minimum deposit amount. Distinct from `OPERATOR_ROLE` to keep router-level configuration separate from vault and strategy operations. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### Adapter

A contract that bridges the [StrategyManager](/protocol-architecture/strategy-manager) to an external yield protocol. Tumbuh ships two adapters: `GenericERC4626Strategy` (works with any ERC-4626 vault) and `MorphoStrategy` (adds Morpho's reward-claiming logic). See [Adapters](/protocol-architecture/adapters).

### Basis points (bps)

A unit of measurement for percentages. 100 bps equals 1%, 10000 bps equals 100%. Tumbuh uses bps throughout: the swap percentage is bounded between 7000 and 9000 bps, and the maximum performance fee is 3000 bps.

### `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`

The OpenZeppelin `AccessControl` admin role. In Tumbuh it controls high-impact configuration: swapping the strategy manager, setting the treasury, wiring the router, and authorizing UUPS upgrades. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### ERC-4626

The Ethereum standard for tokenized vaults. The TumbuhVault implements ERC-4626; Tumbuh strategies plug into external ERC-4626 vaults. See [EIP-4626](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626).

### ERC-7201

The Ethereum standard for namespaced storage layouts in upgradeable contracts. Tumbuh uses ERC-7201 to keep storage layouts stable across upgrades. See [EIP-7201](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7201) and [Upgradeability](/security/upgradeability).

### `GUARDIAN_ROLE`

A role with pause authority on the Vault, StrategyManager, and Router, and the authority to call `emergencyWithdrawStrategy`. See [Pausability and emergency](/security/pausability-emergency).

### High-watermark (HWM)

The all-time-high `totalAssets()` value the Vault has reached. Performance fees only apply to profit *above* the HWM; drawdowns never trigger a fee. The HWM resets upward on every successful `collectFee` call. See [Fees and yield](/fees-and-yield).

### IDRX

A Rupiah-pegged stablecoin, 2 decimals. The current entry asset for Tumbuh deposits. See [Tokens](/tokens#idrx).

### `OPERATOR_ROLE`

A role with day-to-day operational authority on the Vault and StrategyManager: collect performance fees, add or remove strategies, rebalance, and claim-and-compound rewards. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### Receipt token

A token that represents a deposit position without rebasing or accruing yield in the holder's wallet. tIDRX is a receipt token: its balance does not rise; the underlying value lives in vault shares held by the Router on the holder's behalf.

### Share price

`totalAssets() / totalSupply()` for the Vault. As yield accrues, `totalAssets()` rises while `totalSupply()` stays flat (outside of fee mints), so the share price rises. Each share is worth more USDC over time. See [Fees and yield](/fees-and-yield).

### Slippage

The difference between the expected and actual price when assets move through an external market. Tumbuh users encounter slippage during the IDRX↔USDC swap and during external strategy redemptions.

### Slippage isolation

A property of Tumbuh's withdrawal accounting: when you redeem, any slippage incurred while closing your portion of the strategy positions is paid from your proceeds, not shared with other vault holders. This keeps share price stable for holders who did not initiate a withdrawal. Worked example in [Fees and yield](/fees-and-yield).

### Soulbound token

An ERC-20 that cannot be transferred between non-zero addresses. tIDRX is soulbound: only mint (from `address(0)`) and burn (to `address(0)`) are allowed. The Router enforces this in `_update`.

### StrategyManager

The contract that allocates vault USDC across active strategies, rebalances toward target percentages, and triggers reward claiming. Up to 10 strategies can be active at once. See [Strategy manager](/protocol-architecture/strategy-manager).

### SwapContract

A thin wrapper around the 0x API v2 AllowanceHolder. The Router and StrategyManager call it for IDRX↔USDC and reward-token-to-USDC swaps. See [Swap contract](/protocol-architecture/swap-contract).

### tIDRX

The Tumbuh receipt token. Soulbound, 2 decimals, minted 1:1 with the gross IDRX you deposit. Its balance never rises — yield lives in vault shares held by the Router. You see the yield at redemption when tIDRX burns for more IDRX than you deposited. See [Tokens](/tokens#tidrx).

### TumbuhRouter

The user-facing entry point. Splits IDRX between a swap-to-USDC path and an idle balance, deposits the USDC into the Vault, and mints tIDRX as a receipt. The Router holds vault shares on behalf of users. See [Router](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router).

### TumbuhVault

The ERC-4626 vault denominated in USDC. Holds idle USDC and delegates allocation to the StrategyManager. See [Vault](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-vault).

### Universal Rewards Distributor (URD)

Morpho's contract for distributing reward tokens via Merkle proofs. The MorphoStrategy adapter calls it during `claimAndCompound`. See [Integrations](/integrations#morpho).

### USDC

The vault's accounting asset, 6 decimals on Base. The integrated yield protocols denominate in USDC, so the Vault matches them. See [Tokens](/tokens#usdc).

### UUPS

A proxy upgrade pattern where the upgrade logic lives in the implementation contract itself, not the proxy. All Tumbuh core contracts use UUPS. See [OpenZeppelin UUPS](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/api/proxy#UUPSUpgradeable) and [Upgradeability](/security/upgradeability).
