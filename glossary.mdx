# Glossary

Terms specific to Tumbuh are defined here. For canonical industry terms — ERC-4626, ERC-7201, UUPS — this page links to the authoritative source rather than restating the definition.

Entries are alphabetical.

### `ADMIN_MANAGER`

A role on the [TumbuhRouter](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router) that controls the entry-layer parameters. Distinct from `OPERATOR_ROLE` to keep entry-layer configuration separate from vault and strategy operations. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### Adapter

A contract that bridges the [StrategyManager](/protocol-architecture/strategy-manager) to an external yield protocol. Tumbuh ships a generic ERC-4626 adapter and a specialised one for protocols that pay rewards through a Merkle distributor. See [Adapters](/protocol-architecture/adapters).

### Basis points (bps)

A unit of measurement for percentages. 100 bps equals 1%, 10000 bps equals 100%.

### `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`

The OpenZeppelin `AccessControl` admin role. In Tumbuh it controls high-impact configuration: swapping the strategy manager, setting the treasury, wiring the router, and authorizing UUPS upgrades. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### ERC-4626

The Ethereum standard for tokenized vaults. The TumbuhVault implements ERC-4626; Tumbuh strategies plug into external ERC-4626 vaults. See [EIP-4626](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626).

### ERC-7201

The Ethereum standard for namespaced storage layouts in upgradeable contracts. Tumbuh uses ERC-7201 to keep storage layouts stable across upgrades. See [EIP-7201](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7201) and [Upgradeability](/security/upgradeability).

### `GUARDIAN_ROLE`

A role with pause authority on the vault, strategy allocator, and entry layer, and the authority to force-exit a misbehaving yield protocol. See [Pausability and emergency](/security/pausability-emergency).

### High-watermark (HWM)

**Coming soon.** Part of the previous performance-fee design, currently retired. **No performance fee is charged today.** This entry will be updated when the new fee model is ready.

### IDRX

A stablecoin issued by an external project. The current entry asset for Tumbuh deposits, 2 decimals on Base. See [Tokens](/tokens#idrx).

### `OPERATOR_ROLE`

A role with day-to-day operational authority on the vault and strategy allocator: add or remove yield protocols, rebalance, and trigger reward claiming. See [Access control](/security/access-control).

### Receipt token

A token that represents a deposit position without rebasing or accruing yield in the holder's wallet. tIDRX is the current receipt token: its balance does not rise; the underlying value lives in the vault held by the protocol on the holder's behalf.

### Share value

The vault's value divided by the share count. As yield accrues, the value rises while the share count is unchanged outside fee mints (none today), so each share is worth more of the vault asset over time. See [Yield generation](/how-it-works/yield-generation).

### Slippage

The difference between the expected and actual price when assets move through an external market. Tumbuh users encounter slippage during the deposit-asset ↔ vault-asset swap and during yield-protocol redemptions.

### Slippage isolation

A property of Tumbuh's withdrawal accounting: when you withdraw, any cost incurred while closing your portion of the yield-protocol positions is paid from your proceeds, not shared with other vault holders. This keeps share value stable for holders who did not initiate a withdrawal. See [Withdraw flow](/how-it-works/withdraw-flow#why-your-output-may-differ-slightly-from-the-estimate).

### Soulbound token

An ERC-20 that cannot be transferred between non-zero addresses. tIDRX is soulbound: only mint (from `address(0)`) and burn (to `address(0)`) are allowed.

### StrategyManager

The contract that allocates the vault's capital across active yield protocols, rebalances toward target allocations, and triggers reward claiming. Up to 10 yield protocols can be active at once. See [Strategy manager](/protocol-architecture/strategy-manager).

### SwapContract

The thin swap layer that connects Tumbuh to external markets. The router and the strategy allocator call it to swap between assets through an external swap aggregator (currently 0x). See [Swap contract](/protocol-architecture/swap-contract).

### tIDRX

The Tumbuh receipt token (current). Soulbound, 2 decimals, minted 1:1 with the gross amount you deposit. Its balance never rises — yield lives in the vault held by the protocol. You see the yield at withdrawal when tIDRX burns for more than you deposited. See [Tokens](/tokens#tidrx).

### TumbuhRouter

The user-facing entry layer. Splits your deposit asset between a swap-to-vault-asset path and an idle balance, places the swapped portion into the vault, and issues the receipt token. Holds vault positions on behalf of users. See [Router](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router).

### TumbuhVault

The vault, denominated in the vault's current underlying asset (USDC). Holds idle vault asset and delegates allocation to the strategy allocator. See [Vault](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-vault).

### Universal Rewards Distributor (URD)

Morpho's contract for distributing reward tokens via Merkle proofs. The MorphoStrategy adapter calls it when compounding rewards back into the vault asset.

### USDC

The vault's accounting asset, 6 decimals on Base. The vault is currently denominated in USDC to match the integrated yield protocols' denomination. See [Tokens](/tokens#usdc).

### UUPS

A proxy upgrade pattern where the upgrade logic lives in the implementation contract itself, not the proxy. All Tumbuh core contracts use UUPS. See [OpenZeppelin UUPS](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/api/proxy#UUPSUpgradeable) and [Upgradeability](/security/upgradeability).
