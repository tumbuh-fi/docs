# Yield generation

Tumbuh earns yield by deploying USDC across multiple ERC-4626 yield protocols. The earnings flow back to depositors through a single mechanism: the Vault's share price rises over time. There are no airdrops, no claim transactions, and no rebasing balances.

## Share price

The Vault is ERC-4626. Its share price is:

```
share price = totalAssets() / totalSupply()
```

When yield accrues, `totalAssets()` rises while `totalSupply()` is unchanged outside of fee mints, so the share price rises. The same number of shares now represents more USDC.

| Time | `totalAssets()` | `totalSupply()` | Share price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 100.00 USDC | 100 shares | 1.0000 USDC/share |
| Day 30 | 100.42 USDC | 100 shares | 1.0042 USDC/share |
| Day 90 | 101.27 USDC | 100 shares | 1.0127 USDC/share |
| Day 365 | 105.15 USDC | 100 shares | 1.0515 USDC/share |

The numbers above are illustrative. Actual yield depends on what the integrated strategies earn.

## What `totalAssets()` includes

`totalAssets()` returns the Vault's total USDC value, calculated as:

- **Idle USDC** sitting in the Vault contract, plus
- **The current value of every active strategy**, computed by asking each adapter for its `convertToAssets(vault, totalShares)`.

The strategy total uses a fallback wrapper: if an adapter's `convertToAssets` call reverts — for example, because an external vault is paused or upgraded — the value falls back to the strategy's `lastKnownValue` rather than reverting the whole `totalAssets()` call. One broken external integration cannot brick TVL reads or block redemptions on healthy strategies.

## Where the yield comes from

The integrated protocols are Morpho, AutoFinance, Avantis, and any other ERC-4626 vault Tumbuh adds in the future. Each one earns yield through its own mechanics — lending, looped strategies, market making, or otherwise. Tumbuh aggregates the result into a single share price.

Yield reaches the Vault in two ways:

1. **Native ERC-4626 share appreciation.** Most strategies grow their share price natively (Morpho, AutoFinance). The Vault's call to `convertToAssets` returns more USDC over time, so `totalAssets()` rises automatically.
2. **Reward token distributions.** Some strategies pay rewards in a separate token (Morpho pays MORPHO via the [Universal Rewards Distributor](/glossary#universal-rewards-distributor-urd)). These are claimed and converted back to USDC through `claimAndCompound` on the StrategyManager — which the Operator triggers periodically. After compounding, the new USDC sits in the strategy and lifts the share price.

## Rebalancing

The StrategyManager can rebalance toward target percentages by calling `rebalance()`. Rebalance runs in two phases: pull from over-allocated strategies, then redeploy to under-allocated ones. The Operator triggers it when allocations drift, when target percentages change, or when a strategy is added or removed.

Rebalance does not generate yield. It keeps the allocation aligned to the target.

## tIDRX and yield

<Note>
**Your tIDRX balance does not rise as yield accrues.** tIDRX is a receipt token — neither cToken-style (constant balance, rising token value) nor aToken-style (rising balance, pegged value). It is a pure receipt that confirms how much IDRX you originally deposited.

Your yield lives in the vault shares the Router holds on your behalf. You see it at redemption time, when your tIDRX burns for more IDRX than you deposited.
</Note>

If you want a real-time estimate of your position value, query the Router and Vault directly:

```
shares = router.getUserVaultShares(your_address)
usdcValue = vault.convertToAssets(shares)
estimatedIdrx = quote(usdcValue, USDC → IDRX)
totalEstimate = estimatedIdrx + router.getIdleBalance(your_address)
```

A wallet or frontend usually packages this into a single "estimated value" number for you.

## Performance fee

A portion of profit above the Vault's all-time high may be taken as a performance fee. The fee is bounded at **3000 bps (30%)**, paid in vault shares to the treasury, and only applies above the high-watermark — never on drawdowns. See [Fees and yield](/fees-and-yield) for the full mechanics.
