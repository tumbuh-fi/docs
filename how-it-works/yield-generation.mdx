# Yield generation

Tumbuh earns yield by spreading the vault's assets across multiple DeFi yield protocols. The earnings flow back to depositors through a single mechanism: the vault's value rises over time. There are no airdrops, no claim transactions, and no rebasing balances.

## Share value rises over time

Yield raises the vault's value. Because the number of vault shares is fixed (outside the rare fee mint), each share grows in value as the integrated protocols pay yield back to the vault.

| Time | Vault value | Shares | Value per share (USDC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 100.00 USDC | 100 | 1.0000 |
| Day 30 | 100.42 USDC | 100 | 1.0042 |
| Day 90 | 101.27 USDC | 100 | 1.0127 |
| Day 365 | 105.15 USDC | 100 | 1.0515 |

The numbers above are illustrative. Actual yield depends on what the integrated protocols earn.

## How the vault values itself

The vault tracks two things in its accounting: idle assets sitting in the vault and the current value of every active yield-protocol position. If a single integrated protocol is temporarily unable to report its value, the vault falls back to the last known value for that position rather than failing the read for everyone — see [Invariants](/security/invariants) for the auditor-facing detail.

## Where the yield comes from

Tumbuh aggregates yield from a diversified set of DeFi protocols. Each one earns yield on its own terms — lending, looped strategies, market making, or other mechanics — and Tumbuh combines the results into a single rising share value.

Some protocols pay rewards in a separate token. The protocol periodically claims those rewards and converts them back to the vault asset, which lifts share value alongside the underlying yield.

## Rebalancing

As yield accrues at different rates across protocols, the vault occasionally re-balances toward target allocations. Rebalancing happens automatically and does not change your position value — only how it is distributed across protocols.

## Receipt tokens and yield

<Note>
**Your receipt-token balance does not rise as yield accrues.** The receipt token is a pure receipt that confirms how much you originally deposited.

Your yield lives in the vault. You see it at redemption time, when your receipt burns for more than you deposited.
</Note>

The web app at [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi) displays your receipt-token balance — the amount you originally deposited. Yield does not appear in your wallet or in the app while your position is open; it is realised at withdrawal, when your receipt burns for more deposit asset than you originally deposited.
