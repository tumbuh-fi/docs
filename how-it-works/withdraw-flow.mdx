## Partial withdrawals

A partial withdrawal redeems a proportional slice of everything: a slice of your vault position and a matching slice of your idle balance. There is no all-or-nothing constraint and no withdrawal queue.

## Why your output may differ slightly from the estimate

Two sources contribute to the difference between the estimate and the actual amount received:

- **Swap rate movement.** The vault-asset → deposit-asset swap executes at the live market rate. The rate at execution may differ slightly from the rate when you reviewed the estimate.
- **Yield-protocol exit cost.** When the vault redeems part of its position, an integrated protocol may pay out slightly less than its quoted value because of withdrawal fees, looped-position unwinding, or rounding.

Tumbuh isolates the second cost to the user who triggered it: only your proceeds are reduced, not other holders' position value. This keeps the system fair — the user who triggered the exit pays the cost of that exit, and other users are unaffected.

## Worked example

> *Example using IDRX as the deposit asset. Other supported deposit assets follow the same flow.*
>
> *Exchange rates fluctuate. Examples on this page use a reference rate of approximately 17,252.70 IDR/USD (April 2026); your actual amounts vary with market conditions.*

A user holding ~50% of the vault initiates a full withdrawal on app.tumbuh.fi. Their slice estimates approximately **1,000 USDC** of vault assets. With ~0.3% exit cost, the actual amount received is **~997 USDC**. That converts to roughly **17.2 million IDRX** at the reference rate, plus the user's idle balance. Other holders' position value is unchanged.

## Slippage protection

The web app pre-fills a sensible slippage default for the entire round trip. If you want to set it manually, 0.5%–1% is the standard range.

<Warning>
A slippage bound set too tight will cause the transaction to revert and you will pay gas without withdrawing. Setting it too loose accepts more cost from market movement. Most users do not need to change the default — review the value the app proposes before signing.
</Warning>

## If withdrawals are paused

In the rare case the protocol is paused for safety reasons, withdrawals on app.tumbuh.fi are temporarily blocked. The protocol's recovery procedure is documented in [Risks and disclosures](/security/risks-and-disclosures#router-recovery-runbook). Pause is reversible — when normal operation resumes, you can withdraw as usual.
