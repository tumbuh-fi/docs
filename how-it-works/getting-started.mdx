# Getting started

Open [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi), connect an EVM-compatible Web3 wallet, and deposit a supported asset. This page walks through your first deposit and how to keep an eye on your position afterward.

## About your receipt token

Your receipt token is a confirmation of how much you originally deposited — minted 1:1 with the gross deposit. Your yield lives in the vault, not in the receipt. The receipt's balance does not rise as yield accrues; you see your yield at withdrawal, when the receipt burns for more than you deposited.

## Setting slippage

The web app pre-fills a sensible slippage default for you. If you want to set it manually, the standard range is 0.5% to 1%. Tighter bounds increase the chance of a failed transaction; looser bounds accept more cost from market movement.
