# Getting started

Open [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi), connect a Base wallet, and deposit a supported stablecoin (currently IDRX). This page walks through what you need, what to expect during your first deposit, and how to keep an eye on your position afterward.

## What you need

- **A Base-compatible wallet.** Any wallet that supports Base mainnet works. Make sure your wallet is connected to Base, not Ethereum mainnet.
- **A small amount of ETH on Base** to pay gas. A few cents is usually enough.
- **A balance of a supported stablecoin.** The current entry asset is IDRX — see the [IDRX project documentation](https://idrx.co/) for how to acquire and bridge it to Base.

<Tip>
If you have IDRX on a different chain, bridge it to Base before depositing. Tumbuh only accepts the Base IDRX contract.
</Tip>

## What you see afterward

- Your wallet balance of the deposit stablecoin (IDRX) decreases.
- Your wallet balance of the receipt token (tIDRX) increases — 1:1 with the gross amount you deposited.
- Your live position value is visible on [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi) at any time.

<Note>
**Your receipt-token balance does not rise as yield accrues.** The receipt is a confirmation of how much stablecoin you originally deposited; your yield lives in the vault and you see it at withdrawal — when your receipt burns for more stablecoin than you deposited.
</Note>

## Setting slippage

The web app pre-fills a sensible slippage default for you. If you want to set it manually, the standard range is 0.5% to 1%. Tighter bounds increase the chance of a failed transaction; looser bounds accept more cost from market movement.
