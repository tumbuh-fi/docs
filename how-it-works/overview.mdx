# How it works

Tumbuh is a three-step loop:

1. **Deposit.** You send your stablecoin. The protocol swaps most of it to the vault's underlying asset, places the result into the vault, and issues your receipt token.
2. **Earn.** The vault spreads its capital across diversified DeFi yield protocols. Yield raises the vault's share value over time.
3. **Withdraw.** You burn your receipt. The protocol redeems your share of the vault, swaps the proceeds back to your stablecoin, and sends them to you alongside the idle balance the protocol kept aside on your behalf.

## The full flow

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor User
    participant Router as TumbuhRouter
    participant Swap as Swap
    participant Vault as TumbuhVault
    participant SM as Strategy allocator
    participant External as Yield protocols

    User->>Router: Deposit
    Router->>Swap: Swap to vault asset
    Router->>Vault: Place into vault
    Vault->>SM: Allocate across strategies
    SM->>External: Deposit into yield protocols
    Router-->>User: Mint receipt token (1:1 with deposit)
    Note over Router: Idle stablecoin kept per user

    User->>Router: Withdraw
    Router->>Vault: Redeem from vault
    Vault->>SM: Pull from strategies
    SM->>External: Exit yield protocols
    Router->>Swap: Swap back to your stablecoin
    Router-->>User: Stablecoin returned (swap output + idle)
```

## Who holds what

The most non-obvious detail in Tumbuh is that **your vault position is held by the protocol on your behalf, not in your wallet**. Your wallet holds the receipt token, which never changes balance and never moves between addresses.

```mermaid

    subgraph Wallet["Your wallet"]
        TIDRX["Receipt token (1:1 with your deposit)"]
        IDRXBal["Stablecoin balance"]
    end

    subgraph RouterHoldings["Protocol-held (per user)"]
        Shares["Vault position (proportional share)"]
        IdleIDRX["Idle stablecoin (unswapped portion)"]
    end

    subgraph VaultHoldings["Vault holdings"]
        USDCIdle["Idle vault asset"]
        StratPos["Yield-protocol positions"]
    end

    Wallet -.tracks.-> RouterHoldings
    RouterHoldings -.represents.-> VaultHoldings
```

<Info>
The web app at [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi) reads your full position automatically — including the share of vault assets attributed to your address and any idle stablecoin held on your behalf. You do not need to read the on-chain records yourself.
</Info>

## A worked example

> *Example using IDRX as the entry stablecoin. Other supported stablecoins follow the same flow.*
>
> *Exchange rates fluctuate. Examples on this page use a reference rate of approximately 17,252.70 IDR/USD (April 2026); your actual amounts vary with market conditions.*

A short version (full numerical walkthrough lives on the [deposit flow](/how-it-works/deposit-flow) page):

- You deposit **100,000 IDRX**.
- With a swap portion of about 80%, the protocol swaps **80,000 IDRX** to the vault's underlying asset (currently USDC) and keeps **20,000 IDRX** idle on your behalf.
- At a market rate of roughly 17,252.70 IDR/USD, the swap returns approximately **4.64 USDC** before slippage.
- The vault asset enters the vault and is spread across the active yield protocols.
- Your wallet now holds **100,000 tIDRX**. Your full position is the vault share held on your behalf plus the 20,000 IDRX idle balance.

When you redeem the full 100,000 tIDRX later, the protocol pulls a proportional share of the vault, swaps it back to IDRX, and returns the result alongside the 20,000 IDRX idle balance. If yield has accrued, you receive more IDRX than you deposited.
