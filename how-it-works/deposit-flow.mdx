## What happens behind the scenes

When you confirm the deposit, the protocol does three things in a single transaction:

1. **Swap the larger portion to the vault asset.** A configured fraction of your deposit (currently around 80%) is swapped to the vault asset through a market-best-execution route. The remainder is held as your idle balance — to make your eventual withdrawal cheaper.
2. **Place the swapped portion into the vault.** The vault routes it into a diversified set of yield protocols. Your portion is recorded against your address.
3. **Issue the receipt token.** You receive a non-transferable receipt token, 1:1 with the gross amount you deposited. The receipt is your proof of deposit; the actual yield-bearing position is the vault's record of your share.

## Worked example

> *Example using IDRX as the deposit asset. Other supported deposit assets follow the same flow.*
>
> *Exchange rates fluctuate. Examples on this page use a reference rate of approximately 17,252.70 IDR/USD (April 2026); your actual amounts vary with market conditions.*

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| You deposit | **100,000 IDRX** |
| Swapped to vault asset (~80%) | **80,000 IDRX** |
| Held as your idle balance (~20%) | **20,000 IDRX** |
| Quoted vault asset for the swap | **≈ 4.64 USDC** |
| Actual vault asset received (after ~0.5% slippage) | **≈ 4.62 USDC** |
| Receipt token minted to you | **100,000 tIDRX** |

The vault asset received enters the vault and is spread across the active yield protocols. The 20,000 IDRX stays as your idle balance for cheap withdrawal later. Your receipt token confirms your position; its balance does not rise as yield accrues — see [Yield generation](/how-it-works/yield-generation).

## Slippage protection

The web app picks a sensible slippage default for you (a percentage below the live quote that the transaction is allowed to deviate before it reverts). If you want to set it manually, 0.5%–1% is the standard range.

<Tip>
Tighter bounds increase the chance of a reverted transaction; looser bounds accept more cost from market movement. Most users do not need to change the default.
</Tip>

## Common pitfalls

- **Stale quote.** Quotes have a short expiration window. The web app fetches a fresh quote when you press confirm; if you wait too long after that, refresh and try again.
- **Wrong network.** Make sure your wallet is on the network where Tumbuh is deployed (currently Base mainnet).
- **Insufficient deposit-asset balance.** Re-check your balance before signing — the transaction will revert if you do not have enough to cover the deposit.
- **Paused state.** In the rare case the protocol is paused for safety reasons, deposits are temporarily blocked. Pause is reversible; try again when normal operation resumes.
