# TumbuhRouter

The TumbuhRouter is the entry point a regular user interacts with. It accepts the supported stablecoin (currently IDRX), splits each deposit between a swap-to-vault-asset path and an idle reserve, places the swapped portion into [TumbuhVault](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-vault) on your behalf, and issues your receipt token (currently tIDRX). On withdrawal, it does the inverse.

The router is the **extensibility point for additional supported stablecoins**. Today it handles IDRX; the contract surface is shaped so a future router (or this router upgraded) can accept a different supported stablecoin without changes to the underlying vault.

## What the router does

- Pulls your stablecoin from your wallet on deposit.
- Swaps the configured portion to the vault's underlying asset; holds the rest as your per-user idle balance.
- Places the swapped portion into the vault on your behalf, recording the resulting vault position against your address.
- Issues your receipt token 1:1 with the gross stablecoin amount you deposited.
- Reverses the entire flow on withdrawal: burns your receipt, redeems your share of the vault, swaps the proceeds back to your stablecoin, and returns the result to your wallet alongside your idle balance.

## What the router does not do

- It does not custody vault assets directly — that is the vault's job.
- It does not allocate capital across yield protocols — that is the strategy allocator's job.
- It does not collect fees — that is the vault's job.

## Important caveats

<Note>
**The router holds vault positions on behalf of users.** The web app at [app.tumbuh.fi](https://app.tumbuh.fi) reads the per-user record automatically; you do not need to query the vault directly.
</Note>

<Note>
**The receipt-token wiring is fixed at deployment** and cannot be migrated by an admin.
</Note>

<Note>
**The router can be paused** by the protocol's guardian role in an emergency. While paused, deposits and withdrawals are blocked. See [Pausability and emergency](/security/pausability-emergency).
</Note>
