# TumbuhVault

The TumbuhVault is the protocol's accounting engine. It tracks the vault asset, issues vault positions to its depositors, and forwards capital to the strategy allocator that distributes it across yield protocols.

The vault is intentionally narrow. It does not manage strategies, swap tokens, or track per-user positions — those are other components' jobs. Its responsibilities are share accounting and forwarding capital to the strategy allocator. Performance fees are charged per-user in the [TumbuhRouter](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router).

## What the vault does

- Receives the vault asset from the router and credits a vault position for each deposit.
- Forwards capital to the strategy allocator immediately on deposit.
- On redemption, pulls the needed amount from the strategy allocator and returns the actual amount received to the caller.
- Reports the vault's current value (idle assets plus active yield-protocol positions) for the app and integrators.

## Important caveats

<Note>
**The vault tracks each share's value as the integrated protocols pay yield.** There is no rebasing of token balances; the share value rises while the share count stays fixed.
</Note>

<Note>
**The vault uses inflation-attack protection** so an early actor cannot skew the share value through a small donation. See [Invariants](/security/invariants#erc-4626-inflation-attack-protection) for the auditor-facing detail.
</Note>

<Note>
**The vault is not directly callable by regular users.** Deposit and redemption go through the [TumbuhRouter](/protocol-architecture/tumbuh-router).
</Note>
